# Credits & artwork terms

## Distribution logos

`assets/logos/*.txt` and the corresponding generated constants in `src/logo.rs`
are ASCII adaptations of the source artwork below, made for purefetch in 2026.
Contours are sampled onto a 40-column, at most 20-row character grid. Colors are
quantized; white internal details may become negative space, and neutral marks
are lightened for dark terminals. These adaptations are not official brand assets.

The program's MIT/Apache-2.0 licenses do not relicense this artwork. The original
creators retain their rights. Project names and logos identify the reported
operating system and do not imply affiliation with or endorsement of purefetch.
Trademark rights are not granted by the software or artwork licenses.
